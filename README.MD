# Project Altitude

> Designing a calmer, thumb-first flight booking experience.

---

## Overview

Project Altitude is a reimagining of the flight booking experience.

Instead of overwhelming users with filters, offers, upsells, and endless flight comparisons, the product focuses on helping travellers complete one task effortlessly:

> Find the right flight and book it with confidence.

The design philosophy is built around simplicity, progressive disclosure, and recommendation-driven decisions rather than traditional search-driven interfaces.

---

# Vision

Booking a flight should feel as effortless as sending a message.

Instead of asking users to configure dozens of search parameters before showing anything useful, the interface progressively guides them through each decision.

The experience reduces cognitive load by exposing only what is necessary at every step.

---

# Design Principles

## Simplicity First

Every screen should have one primary purpose.

Avoid presenting multiple competing actions.

---

## Progressive Disclosure

Only ask for information when it becomes necessary.

Users should never feel like they're filling out a form.

---

## Recommendation over Inventory

Traditional OTAs show thousands of flights.

Project Altitude recommends journeys.

The product should help users decide instead of forcing them to compare everything.

---

## Thumb-first Interaction

Most interactions happen in the bottom third of the screen.

Instead of forcing users to reach the top repeatedly, the interface is designed around natural thumb movement.

Examples include:

- Bottom search
- Upward-expanding lists
- Persistent bottom actions

---

## Familiar Patterns

Innovation should reduce friction, not create it.

Well-understood interaction patterns like calendars and searchable lists remain intact.

The intelligence is layered on top of familiar interfaces instead of replacing them.

---

# MVP Scope

The MVP focuses only on booking flights.

## Home

- Origin
- Destination
- Travel dates
- Passenger selection

---

## Airport Search

- Search-first experience
- Bottom-anchored search
- Upward-growing airport list
- Recent airports
- Popular airports
- Full searchable airport directory

---

## Date Selection

Traditional calendar enhanced with:

- Cheapest dates
- Fare trends
- Smart recommendations

The calendar remains familiar while providing better decision support.

---

## Flight Recommendations

Instead of presenting endless search results, users receive curated recommendations such as:

- Best Value
- Cheapest
- Fastest

Each recommendation clearly explains why it was chosen.

---

## Booking

Simple checkout.

Minimal distractions.

No aggressive upselling.

---

# Out of Scope

The following ideas are intentionally excluded from the MVP.

- Hotels
- Cabs
- Activities
- AI travel assistant
- Journey timeline
- Offline travel support
- Travel wallet
- Loyalty management
- Visa assistance
- Expense tracking
- Shared itineraries
- Physical travel card
- Travel OS

These will be introduced only after the flight booking experience feels complete.

---

# Product Philosophy

Most travel apps optimise for transactions.

Project Altitude optimises for confidence.

Success is measured by how quickly and comfortably a traveller can decide.

The goal is not to show more choices.

The goal is to make choosing easier.

---

# Long-term Direction

The MVP is only the foundation.

Future versions will gradually evolve from:

Flight Booking

↓

Journey Management

↓

Travel Companion

↓

Travel Operating System

Every new capability must follow the same principles established by the MVP:

- Calm
- Simple
- Context-aware
- Recommendation-driven
- Human-first

Complexity should never be exposed to the user unless it provides immediate value.

---

# Success Criteria

The MVP succeeds if users can:

- Find a destination quickly
- Select dates confidently
- Understand recommended flights immediately
- Complete booking with minimal effort
- Feel less overwhelmed than using traditional OTAs

If these goals are achieved, additional travel services can be layered onto the platform without compromising the experience.
